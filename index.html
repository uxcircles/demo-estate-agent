FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I buy a home in the UK on a BN(O) or work visa?

Yes. There is no visa requirement to own property in the UK. Getting a mortgage depends on the lender — some specialise in recent arrivals — so we suggest speaking to a mortgage broker early.

Do I pay extra Stamp Duty as a new arrival?

Stamp Duty Land Tax depends on the price and your circumstances. Buyers who are not UK resident for tax purposes may pay an extra surcharge. Your conveyancer will confirm what applies to you.

I have no UK credit history. Can I still rent?

Usually, yes. Landlords may ask for a UK guarantor or proof of income or savings instead. We explain the options before you apply.

How much is the holding deposit when renting?

In England, a holding deposit is capped at one week’s rent, and a tenancy deposit at five weeks’ rent for annual rent under £50,000. Deposits must be protected in a government-approved scheme.
